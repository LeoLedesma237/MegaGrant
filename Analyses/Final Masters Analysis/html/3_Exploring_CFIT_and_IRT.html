Reading in the CFIT data

The CFIT Scale 2 Form B is a timed non-verbal fluid intelligence test. It contains 46 items and 4-subscales:

  • Series (12 items)
  • Classifications (14 items)
  • Matrices (12 items)
  • Conditions (8 items)

Running a 2PL, 4-factor 2PL, and Bi-Factor 2PL Model on the CFIT Data

We chose not to fit a hierarhical model because a) it failed to converge (producing an error) and b) theoretically it is not that good of a representation of IQ.

From the model comparison below, the Bi-Factor model produced the best fit (lowest AIC & BIC)

We don’t care for the likelihood ratio tests below- what we are interested in is the AIC and BIC values, in which we see that the bi-factor model is the best fitting model for the data.
